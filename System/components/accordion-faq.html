What is Bland AI?
Bland is a platform for building and deploying phone-calling AI agents at enterprise scale.

You pay a flat per-minute talk-time rate with no token or model pass-through charges.
How long does it take to deploy an agent?
Most teams go from zero to a production agent in under a week.
How does Bland handle HIPAA, PCI, and SOC 2?
Bland is built self-hosted-first so regulated data never has to leave your infrastructure.
